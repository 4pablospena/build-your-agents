This is the example post shipped with the site. It exists so you can verify **listings**, **tags**, **search**, **pagination**, and **reading time** without writing your own content first.

## Why a blog here

The main site documents a **seven-file architecture** for personal agents. The blog is the place for **longer narratives**: trade-offs, updates, and field notes that do not belong inside `SOUL.md` or `AGENTS.md`.

## What comes next

Future posts will walk through each layer of the spec, with concrete examples and mistakes included. If something is unclear, that is signal to tighten the templates—not to add mystery.

### A small checklist

- Keep posts **dated** and **tagged** for discoverability.
- Use `draft: true` until you are ready to publish (drafts only show in development).
- Prefer short paragraphs; the layout is tuned for **readability** on small screens.

Thank you for reading.
